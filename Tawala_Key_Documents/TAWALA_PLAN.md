Tawala Tests is a one-stop place for individuals, groups and organizations to find and put to use a wide variety of tests to evaluate skills, emotional makeup, physical health, and general competencies, either for themselves or for evaluation of clients, jobseekers, or other potential affiliates. People can discover assessment tests that measure everything from the senses to physical fitness, cognitive and memory skills, social and personality types, intuition, empathy, belief systems, information assessment ability, sexuality, skills and competencies, fitness for specific vocations or life goals. Testing and other tools are also available for groups assessing and managing social interactions, group organization and scheduling, HR hiring and evaluation, and a variety of crowdsourcing activities such and polling, petitioning, fundraising and political campaign tools.

Many of these tools are free. Some are branded and carry a cost to use, clearly marked. All the tests are vetted by experts and by you, the users. Comments about these tests and other projects are available to guide users to the most appropriate tools (and to guide the company in sourcing or improving better products). Registered users can track their progress over time, whether it's in learning a language, achieving personal fitness goals, or tracking a degenerative disease.

Tawala's library of testing and other organizational tools (called "Projects") come from a variety of sources including internal development. The Library is divided into four main sections:

- **All about Me** — designed to help people understand themselves better and achieve specific goals for themselves
- **All about Us** — tools that facilitate human interaction
- **Organizational Tools** — process control systems, membership management, sports club management, HR vetting and hiring
- **Produced by our users** using Tawala Designer — a library of forms, tests and tools developed by our users, evaluated by them and offered for use to all

Tawala Designer is a Web tool, available without cost to all registered users, that permits one to download a form or test (or any other Project) and modify it for their specific use, then deploy it back to a private part of the Library called MyTawala, where it can be offered to friends and clients or used oneself. If one wishes, one can submit it to Tawala for publication in the Produced by our Users section of the library (after a short vetting by the Company to ensure compliance with user standards), where all users can try them out, evaluate them and make suggestions for improvement.

All Projects in all four sections of the Tawala Library are evaluated by users by a reputation system which helps other potential users select Projects of interest to them. Projects within libraries categories can be sorted by rating as well as filtered by popularity, cost (if any) and professional review grade.

The Company is guided by a number of criteria in selecting Projects for publication. One of the main factors is whether the Project is capable of online presentation in the current Tawala instantiation. For example, could an audiology test be offered on-line or would it have to wait for the Tawala system to implement audio input and output capabilities first? Would competency testing in art imagery or higher mathematics have to await implementation of imagery inclusion in Designer questions? Extension of the Tawala Designer will be driven going forward in part by the importance of particular category tests to our users.

Another way in which the Tawala site will have to grow will be in the use of short YouTube-style tutorials for many Projects as well as for the Designer and Library themselves. Such tutorials have been used in the past for some of the Sport Dashboards Projects. Much broader use is anticipated.

In the long run, the most important part of Tawala may well be the quality of overarching cross-Project assessments offered by Tawala tracking metrics over time to evolve predictive evaluations of best possibilities for outcomes for individuals and organizations. These, frankly, are only possible at all because of the rapidly growing capabilities of AI when given adequate information to work with.

The financial model for this plan is complex. It depends upon building a base of users to create virality, buzz and feedback, to determine where interests are most acute and to evaluate the effectiveness of specific tests to produce assessments with perceived value either for users or for organization evaluating users for specific purposes. That most individuals will respond only to a free service is likely. However, organizations for whom those users are clients or members may be open to a paid model. Furthermore, if we can use aggregated information (and it's not clear that we can for much of this), there is a big market for big data. It's possible that there will be a free tier limited by number of Projects one can keep records on in MyTawala with subscriptions available with substantially larger limits, similar to what Google did eventually with Gmail. But until we know costs more precisely it's hard to assess what subscriptions might look like.
